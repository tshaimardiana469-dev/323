About SFSETH

Founded in Manchester, England, SFSETH specializes in independent financial information consulting with strict neutral analysis standards and client-first service principles.

Our Corporate Mission

SFSETH was established in 2010 to break information asymmetry in global financial markets. Our core team consists of senior financial analysts, data scientists and wealth management experts with decades of combined experience across European capital markets. We are committed to providing unbiased, data-driven financial insights to help clients navigate complex market environments.

We strictly separate research and transaction business to guarantee objective data output. All consulting reports, market briefings and asset plans are fully customized according to each customer’s investment goals, holding cycle and capital scale. Our independence ensures that our recommendations are always aligned with the client's best interests, not third-party commissions.
